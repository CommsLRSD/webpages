Opportunity grows here

A stronger community starts with more possibilities for young people.

Established by LRSD in partnership with The Winnipeg Foundation, Riel-Evate brings our community together to help young people access opportunities to learn, participate and thrive.

A commitment that continues

$12,000 in scholarships every year

Each year, Riel-Evate provides eight $1,500 scholarships to graduating LRSD students facing socioeconomic barriers, helping them take their next steps into post-secondary education.

The fund has grown to the point where its endowment helps sustain this annual commitment. Now, we want to build on that success.

More than scholarships

You can help young people even more.

Our goal is to expand Riel-Evate's impact beyond scholarships. With greater community support, Riel-Evate can help reduce the financial barriers that prevent some young people from taking part in programs, activities and experiences that enrich their lives and support their success.

As the fund grows, so does our ability to make more possible for young people across LRSD.

Bring people together

Start your own fundraiser

You don't have to make a donation on your own to make a difference.

Students, staff, schools and members of the community are encouraged to bring people together and organize their own fundraisers in support of Riel-Evate.

That could mean a school event, student-led initiative, community fundraiser or another creative idea. Every effort helps grow what Riel-Evate can do for young people across LRSD.
